PRINTING - 13 SHEETS TOTAL
1. Print print-all-panels-A4.pdf in color on WHITE A4 paper, single-sided.
2. Select Actual size / 100%. Turn OFF Fit, Fill, Shrink, Poster and Booklet.
3. Print one PDF page per sheet. Do not print multiple pages per sheet.
4. Use automatic portrait/landscape orientation:
   - Pages 06, 07 and 13: portrait, 210 x 297 mm.
   - Pages 01-05 and 08-12: landscape, 297 x 210 mm.
5. Borderless printing is unnecessary. Every page has a blank print margin.
6. PNG alternative: portrait images are 2480 x 3508 pixels; landscape images
   are 3508 x 2480 pixels. Use the same A4 orientation and disable cropping.
7. Test page 13 first: its end ticks must be 50 mm apart at 100%.
   Check the QR on a printed page 12 before mounting the final board.

MOUNT THE 12 CONTENT PANELS
Use the numbered assembly guide. Sheets 01 and 02 form the large title.
No letter or graphic is split between those two independent A4 panels.
Mount content panels untrimmed, or trim outside each rounded border.
If trimmed, keep the artwork at the same coordinates as in the full sheets.

The 1-4 badges INSIDE the artwork are story stages, not sheet numbers.
The assembly guide and filenames use sheet numbers 01-13.

The configuration panel is now 05 at the lower left.
The benefits panel is now 10 at the upper right.
Do not use the old 05-benefits / 10-configuration placement.

CUT OUT THE CONNECTORS FROM SHEET 13
Do not mount page 13 as a complete sheet. Cut straight between matching
corner crop marks and discard the marks and headings. The marks lie outside
the exact-size cut-outs used in the preview and placement table.
D and E spares are optional replacements, not additional connectors to mount.

R1 + R2 form ONE dashed rules-to-checkpoint connector.
- R1 is the short tail above the configuration panel on the LEFT WING.
- R2 is the longer diagonal arrow in the CENTER, labelled Rules applied here.
- Keep both pieces in their printed orientation. Do not rotate or resize.
- Their paper edges leave a 4 mm gap centered on the left fold:
  R1 ends at x=302.8 mm and R2 begins at x=306.8 mm.
  The fold is at x=304.8 mm. Leave this hinge clear; do not bridge it.

D is the solid right-pointing arrow: Checkpoint -> Three outcomes.
E is the solid downward arrow: Three outcomes -> Decision receipt.
E says All three outcomes recorded so it does not suggest only blocks
produce a receipt. The receipt is recorded before any allowed execution.

The cut-outs may overlap the BLANK white margins of the A4 content sheets.
Their specified positions do not cover any text, colored container, or icon.
Every visible connector and connector label in the preview comes from page 13.
No separate oversized print or hand-drawn arrow is required.
